For parents

Parents Often Ask

What age can my child start?

Children from 5 to 15 can join. We place each child in a batch that matches their age and current level.

Does my child need an instrument for the trial?

No. Your child can use the academy's instruments in the trial class, and we will guide you on choosing one for home practice.

How does the sibling discount work?

When a second child from the same family enrols, their monthly fee is 10% lower. Mention it at enrolment and our team will apply it.

Can we switch plans later?

Yes. If your child is ready for more attention or a different batch, talk to us and we will help you move to the right plan.
