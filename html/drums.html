Learning Journey

A Step-by-Step Learning Journey

Structured levels to help you progress with confidence.

01

Beginner

  • Grip, posture and kit basics
  • Simple beats and exercises
  • Build timing and coordination
  • Learn to read basic notation
02

Intermediate

  • Rudiments and fills
  • Different time signatures
  • Play a wide range of songs
  • Improve speed and control
03

Advanced

  • Complex grooves and styles
  • Solo and improvisation
  • Playing with a band
  • Prepare for performances
