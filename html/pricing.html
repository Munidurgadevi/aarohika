Quick answers

Pricing Questions

Is the trial class really free?

Yes. Every course has a free trial class with no commitment, so you can meet the instructor and see how classes run.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. The monthly fee covers classes. If extra fees apply, such as exam or event fees, we tell you before you enrol.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a different batch or plan as your goals change. Talk to us and we will help you pick the right one.

How do the quarterly and sibling offers work?

Pay three months in advance and save 15%, or enrol a second child and save 10%. Ask our team to apply the offer.
