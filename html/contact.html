Quick answers

Common Questions

Is the first trial class really free?

Yes. Every course has a free trial class, so you can meet the instructor and experience the academy before you decide.

Do you teach both kids and adults?

Yes. We run lively classes for kids, teenagers and adults, and every lesson is matched to the learner's age and level.

I have never played an instrument. Can I join?

Absolutely. Our beginner batches start from the basics and move step by step, so no prior experience is needed.

Do I need my own instrument?

Use academy instruments during trial; we'll help you choose one for home practice.
