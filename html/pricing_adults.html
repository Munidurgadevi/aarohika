Quick answers

Adult Learners Ask

I have never played an instrument. Can I join?

Absolutely. Our beginner batches start from the very basics, so you do not need any prior experience.

Can I learn around a full-time job?

Yes. Weekend batches and weekday evening batches are designed for working adults. One-on-One lets you choose your own slot.

How does the quarterly saving work?

Pay for three months together and get 15% off your monthly fee. Ask our team to apply it when you enrol.

Do I need to buy an instrument first?

Not for the trial class. We will help you choose a suitable instrument once you decide to continue.
