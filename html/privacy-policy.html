Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Aarohika Music Academy collects from learners and parents, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

On this page

01 Overview

This policy applies when you visit the Aarohika website, book a trial class, enroll in a batch or use the learner dashboard. Many of our learners are children, so we take extra care to collect only what we need and to keep it safe.

By using our services, you agree to the practices described here.

02 Information we collect

We collect the details needed to run classes and keep in touch with you:

♪

Learner details

Name, age group, chosen instrument and weekly plan.

♫

Contact details

Parent or guardian name, mobile number, email and residential address.

♩

Learning activity

Batches joined, attendance, practice progress, feedback notes and class recordings.

♬

Payment details

Fee history and receipts. Payments are handled by secure payment partners, and we do not store full card numbers.

Please note: we only ask for information that helps us teach, schedule and support you.

03 How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Enroll learners and manage batches and schedules.
  • Send class reminders, feedback notes and fee reminders.
  • Process payments and share receipts.
  • Respond to your questions and enquiries.
  • Improve our classes, website and learner dashboard.

04 Children's privacy

For learners under 18, we collect and use information with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian. Parents can review, update or ask us to delete their child's information at any time.

We do not use children's information for advertising, and we do not contact children directly outside of their classes without a parent or guardian being informed.

05 Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Your instructor, so they can plan and teach your classes.
  • Payment partners, to process fees securely.
  • Service providers who help us send messages and run our website.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to.

06 Photos & recordings

Class recordings are shared only with the learner and parent through the dashboard or WhatsApp, for practice. Photos and videos from events or performances are used on our website or social media only with permission from the learner or parent.

You can ask us to stop using or remove any photo or video of your child at any time.

07 Security & retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Access to learner details is limited to the staff and instructors who need it.

We keep your information for as long as you are enrolled and for a reasonable period afterwards, for records and legal needs. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

08 Your choices & rights

You are in control of your information. You can:

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold.
  • Update your contact details in the Profile section of your dashboard.
  • Turn class, WhatsApp and payment notifications on or off in your notification preferences.
  • Withdraw your consent for photos and recordings.

Questions about your privacy?

Write to hello@aarohika.com and we will be happy to help.
