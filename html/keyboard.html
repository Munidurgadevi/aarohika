Learning Journey

A Step-by-Step Learning Journey

Structured levels to help you progress with confidence.

Beginner

  • Notes, keys and finger placement
  • Simple songs and exercises
  • Basic chords and rhythm
  • Learn to read basic notation
02

Intermediate

  • Advanced chords and scales
  • Both-hand coordination
  • Play a wide range of songs
  • Improve speed and accuracy
03

Advanced

  • Music theory for keyboard

  • Prepare for performances
