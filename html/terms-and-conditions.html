01 Acceptance of terms

By enrolling in a batch, booking a trial class or using the Aarohika website and learner dashboard, you agree to these Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree with any part of them, please do not use our services.

For learners under 18, a parent or guardian must accept these terms on the learner's behalf. We may update these terms from time to time, and the latest version always applies to new enrollments.

02 Classes & courses

Aarohika offers music classes for kids and adults in guitar, keyboard, vocals and drums. Depending on the course, this can include:

  • Group batches and private one-to-one classes.
  • Beginner, intermediate and advanced learning levels.
  • Trial classes for new learners.
  • Practice guidance, feedback notes and class recordings through the dashboard.
  • Student performances and events.

Please note: course descriptions on our website are for guidance. Batch timings, instructors and class formats may change, and we will inform you in advance.

03 Enrollment & batches

A trial class helps you meet the instructor and choose a suitable batch. Batch availability is shown as Open or Limited and can change at any time.

Your seat is confirmed once your enrollment is accepted and the fee for the first billing cycle is received. We may move a learner to a more suitable batch if the level or schedule needs to change.

04 Fees & payments

Fees depend on the course and the number of weekly sessions in each billing cycle. Your amount, last payment date and past receipts are available in the Fees & Billing section of your dashboard.

You can pay by UPI, card or bank transfer. Delayed payments may pause your classes until the amount due is cleared.

05 Learner responsibilities

To help every learner progress, we ask learners and parents to:

  • Attend classes on time and come prepared.
  • Practise regularly, as suggested by the instructor.
  • Respect instructors, staff and other learners.
  • Take care of academy instruments and equipment.
  • Keep contact details in the Profile section up to date.

06 Attendance & rescheduling

If a learner cannot attend, please inform the academy before the class. Where possible, the instructor will offer another slot, depending on availability.

We may reschedule a class because of instructor unavailability, holidays or other reasons beyond our control. In that case, we will inform you and arrange a replacement class.

07 Recordings & privacy

Class recordings and feedback notes are shared with learners for practice and are for personal use only. Please do not share them publicly without our permission.

Photos or videos from events and performances are published only with the permission of the learner or parent. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information.

08 Cancellation & refunds

You can leave a batch by informing us before the start of the next billing cycle. Fees for sessions already completed in the current cycle are not refundable.

We may pause or end enrollment if fees remain overdue or these terms are not followed. In that case, we will explain the reason and settle any amount due for the classes completed.
