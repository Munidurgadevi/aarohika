FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our music classes, enrollment process, schedules and more. Still have a question? We're happy to help!

Contact Us
What age groups do you offer classes for?

We offer music classes for kids (5+ years) and adults. Our programs are designed to suit different age groups, skill levels and learning goals.

How can I enroll in a class?

Choose a course, book a free trial or contact us, and our team will help you pick a batch and complete your enrollment in a few easy steps.

Do I need to have a musical instrument?

Not at the start. Use academy instruments during class, and we'll guide you on choosing your instrument when ready to practise home.

What are the class timings?

Classes run Monday to Saturday, 9 AM – 8 PM, with morning, evening and weekend batches so you can pick a time that fits your schedule.

Are there performance opportunities?

Yes! Students can take part in recitals, open mic nights, band jams and our annual concert to build stage confidence.

Do you offer trial classes?

Yes, we offer a free trial class for every course so you can meet our instructors and experience the academy with no commitment.
